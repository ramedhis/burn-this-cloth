burn-this-cloth
---------------
 
An interactive toy that turns an image into a piece of
draggable, burnable cloth. 
 
Hover or move your pointer over the canvas to push and
deform the fabric, then click a spot to set it on fire.
Swipe fast for a wind gust.
 
Made with: PixiJS (WebGL) for rendering, vanilla JS for
the cloth physics and fire simulation.
 
> To use, open [index.html] in a browser.
