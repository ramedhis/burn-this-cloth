burn-this-cloth
A cloth-burning interactive simulation web toy.

OK, SO WHAT CAN YOU DO?

  • Hover anywhere near the cloth to push it. Hold Shift while hovering to disable this.
  • Click a spot to set it on fire. Click and drag to trail fire across the cloth.
  • Hover the cloth to reveal its frame and quick tools. Hold Shift while hovering to hide.
  • Click and drag a frame edge to move the cloth. Hold Shift while dragging to resize instead (works on corners too).
  • Shift+click a point on the cloth to nail an anchor there, or shift+click an existing anchor to remove it.
  • Hold Shift and hover an edge of the cloth for ~1s to highlight it, then click to anchor or unanchor the whole edge.
